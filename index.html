GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Are these products intended to treat or cure a condition?

No. Dietary supplements are not medicines and should not be presented as treatments or cures. Always read the product label and consult a qualified healthcare professional with questions.

Can supplements replace a balanced diet?

No. Supplements are not a replacement for a varied, balanced diet and healthy lifestyle.

How do I know if a product is right for me?

Review the full ingredient list, directions and warnings on the product label. Ask a qualified healthcare professional before use, especially if you take medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a health condition.
